Product Innovation

Paytm Saathi: Voice-First Native Closing

A natural spoken conversation that reconciles the day's financials in under 30 seconds.

Layer 1: Pre-Reconciliation
Soundbox Continuity
Saathi greets the merchant with today's verified Soundbox QR collections fetched directly from the database. It never asks for numbers Paytm already knows.
Layer 2: Guided Voice Flow
3 Conversational Turns
1. Cash collected today?
2. Business expenses (electricity, tea, transport)?
3. Stock purchases (milk packets, staples)?
Accurately extracts numbers from colloquial phrases in Telugu, Hindi, and English.
Layer 3: Instant Ledger
Automated Reconciliation
Calculates Gross Sales (QR + Cash), Net In-Hand Balance, and remaining stock counts. Commits to SQLite records with a single spoken confirmation.
Key Differentiator: Complete daily closing without typing a single digit or navigating menu trees.
Technical Architecture

Engineered for High-Reliability Voice AI

Full-stack pipeline optimized for regional Indian languages with graceful failovers.

01 • Input
Speech-to-Text
Sarvam Saaras v4
Regional Indian speech recognition with dialect robustness and code-mixed (Hinglish/Tenglish) parsing.
02 • Logic
Entity Engine
Hybrid State Machine
Deterministic regex + fuzzy intent mapping to reliably parse cash amounts, expense categories, and quantities without hallucinations.
03 • Output
Voice Synthesis
Sarvam Bulbul v4 Flash
Expressive regional Indian voice synthesis in Telugu, Hindi, and English with conversational prosody.
04 • Storage
ACID Database
FastAPI + SQLite
Persistent ledger storage, stock formulas, daily reconciliation histories, and serverless-ready deployment.
Zero-Permission Prototype Resilience
If hardware microphone access is restricted on mobile webviews or denied by browser security policies, Saathi auto-activates interactive listening mode with one-tap voice response chips so the closing experience never breaks.
